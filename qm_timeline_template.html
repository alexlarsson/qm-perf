Boot timelines and stall evidence

One graph per boot, on the same time axis. Thin dashed lines show recorded service startup and comparison intervals, including healthy starts. Solid bars and dots show the detected observations. Times are seconds since the reconstructed Linux kernel-start origin; operation bounds and report times do not establish the exact onset of a CPU stall.
